Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Grow With English Reels is a private content-publishing application used by authorized administrators of the Grow With English Facebook Page. It helps the Page administrator upload and publish educational English videos through Meta's official platform services.

Information we process

The application may process:

  • Facebook Page name and Page ID.
  • Authorization information provided by Meta.
  • Videos, captions, descriptions, and publishing schedules.
  • Video IDs, publishing status, timestamps, and technical logs.

The application is not intended for public user registration and does not request passwords or payment information from Facebook users.

How information is used

Information is used only to:

  • Upload and publish approved videos to the Facebook Page.
  • Check whether a video has been processed and published.
  • Prevent accidental duplicate publishing.
  • Diagnose technical errors in the publishing workflow.

Storage and security

Authorization credentials are stored privately in the Page administrator's secured automation environment. Credentials are not placed on this website or made publicly available.

Sharing of information

Information is shared only with services required to operate the publishing workflow, including Meta/Facebook and Google services selected by the Page administrator. We do not sell personal information.

Data retention

Publishing records and technical logs are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate and troubleshoot the service. The Page administrator can revoke the application's access through Facebook at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the Grow With English Facebook Page .

Terms of Service

Authorized use

The application may only be used to upload and manage content that the Page administrator owns or has permission to publish.

Limitation

User Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Grow With English Reels is not offered as a public consumer application. However, requests to remove information associated with the application will be honored.

How to request deletion

  1. Visit the Grow With English Facebook Page .
  2. Send a private message containing the words Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include enough information to identify the relevant account or publishing record. Do not send a password or access token.

What will be deleted

After a valid request is confirmed, applicable authorization records, publishing logs, and stored identifiers controlled by the application will be deleted or anonymized within 30 days.

Videos and posts already published on Facebook are hosted by Meta. Those items can be deleted directly through the Grow With English Facebook Page management tools.
